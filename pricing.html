Questions people ask

What does a full search cost?

It depends on the role, so Kaitlin quotes it per search rather than publishing a number that would be wrong half the time. You see it in writing before any work starts.

Can I switch from the board to a full search?

Yes, and people often do when an advert has run a month without the right person. Send the role over and Kaitlin will tell you honestly whether a listing would have been enough.

What happens if the hire doesn't work out?

Every placement made on her recommendation carries a 90-day replacement guarantee, written into your agreement.

Do unused job posts roll over?

Your allowance resets with each billing period. Closing a post frees the slot straight away.

Not sure which you need? Send the role over and we'll tell you honestly — sometimes a listing really is enough.
