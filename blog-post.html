Why your advert has been up for forty days

8 min read · October 2026 · Kaitlin Veltkamp

Every week someone tells me the market is empty. It almost never is. We have two hundred new job seekers a month and twenty-four specialties, and in Ocala you can walk into a feed store and find three barn managers. If nobody is applying, something about the advert is doing it.

Here are the four things that are usually wrong, roughly in the order they are usually wrong.

1. There is no pay on it

This is the big one and it is not close. A listing with a real range gets about three times the applications of one that says “competitive”. Not because people are mercenary — because a missing number reads as a warning. The assumption is always that it is missing for a reason.

“Competitive” is not a range. It is a word people have learned to translate as “less than you want”.

If you genuinely do not know, put the range you could stretch to for the right person and say that is what it is. Honest uncertainty reads far better than silence.

2. Housing is not mentioned

Second only to pay, and for a groom or a working student often ahead of it. People filter on it. If you offer it, say so in the first line. If you do not, say that too — you will lose the applications you were going to lose anyway, but three weeks earlier.

3. It describes the duties but not the days

Most adverts list tasks. Almost none say what a Tuesday looks like, how many horses, who else is on the yard, or whether you compete every weekend through the winter. That is what someone is actually deciding about. The yards that fill roles fastest are the ones that write two honest sentences about the rhythm of the place.

4. It has been up too long, and that is now the problem itself

After about a month a listing starts working against you. Regulars have seen it, scrolled past it, and formed a view — usually that nobody is taking it, and there must be a reason. At that point rewriting it is worth more than extending it.

If you are at forty days, do not renew it. Take it down, fix the three things above, and put it back up as a new post. Or send it to us and we will tell you honestly which of the four it is.
