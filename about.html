About EquiStaff

Forty-two years of knowing who's any good.

From Ocala, Florida — the Horse Capital of the World — placing people from the shed row to the board room.

EquiStaff has been placing people in the horse industry since 1982. That is longer than most barns we work with have existed, and it is the whole of the business: four decades of knowing which yards look after their staff, which managers last, and who is quietly ready to move.

What we actually do

Two things. There is a job board, which is free for anyone looking and a subscription for employers who want to post. And there is the recruiting side — direct hire and RPO — where we take the brief, search our own network, check every reference by phone, and bring back a shortlist of the people genuinely worth meeting — one if one fits, ten if the field is strong.

The second one is the business. The board is how people find us.

Why the phone, every time

An emailed reference tells you somebody had the job. A phone call tells you whether they were any good at it, why they left, and whether the person on the other end would have them back. We have never found a shortcut that works, so we do not take one.

Confidential by default

On a managed search we do not put the barn's name on the advert. Partly that is discretion for the employer. Mostly it is because a candidate who goes around the recruiter costs the barn the vetting and costs us the placement. The name comes out when we introduce you, and not before.
