Equine staffing and recruitment across the United States

EquiStaff is an equine staffing agency based in Ocala, Florida — the Horse Capital of the World. Since 1982 we have connected job seekers and employers across every corner of the horse industry, from show barns and breeding operations to private estates and equestrian centers. We recruit and place staff in all fifty states.

  • Groom and barn staff jobs
