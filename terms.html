The rules, in plain English

Short, because the long version mostly exists to be unread.

Using the site

Browsing is free and open. Applying is free. You need an account to apply, track applications or post a job.

One account per person. Keep your password to yourself, and tell us if you think someone else has it.

If you are looking for work

  • Tell the truth on your profile. Experience we cannot verify is experience we cannot represent.
  • A resume we help you build is yours to check before it is saved. You are responsible for what it says.
  • Do not apply on someone else's behalf without telling us.

We never charge job seekers. Employers pay. If anyone asks you for money to be put forward for a role on this site, that is not us — tell us immediately.

If you are hiring

  • Post real roles with real pay. “Competitive” is not a pay range.
  • Job posts are subject to approval and we can decline or remove one.
  • Your plan gives you a number of posts per period. Unused posts do not roll over.
  • Candidate details are for filling your role. Do not pass them on or add them to a mailing list.

On a managed search, approaching a candidate around us ends the arrangement and the placement fee still falls due. That rule is why employer names stay off managed adverts.

Recruiting work

A managed search is quoted per role in writing before any work starts. The starting fee is due on engagement; the balance is due when your hire starts. Replacement guarantees, where they apply, are set out in that written agreement and not here.

Things we cannot promise

We do not guarantee that you will be hired, or that a role will be filled. We check references carefully and we are honest with you, and that is the whole of what we can promise about another human being.

Some listings come from third-party systems. We show them in good faith but cannot guarantee a role that is not ours is still open.

Your data, and what happens when you leave

You can put your account to sleep whenever you like. Doing that hides your profile from every employer search, withdraws you from anything still open and stops all email from us.

It does not erase your history with EquiStaff. When you give us information — your profile, your applications, what you told the recruiting team you were looking for — we keep it. We keep it so that your account can be woken up with nothing lost, and so that a recruiter who has spoken to you before still knows that when you come back. A staffing agency that forgets you every time you log out is not much use to you.

So: sleep, not delete. If that is not what you want, do not put information here you would need removed later.

Two things sit outside this. Records we are required to keep — placements, invoices, tax and legal records — are kept for as long as the law says, whatever else happens. And if you live somewhere that gives you a legal right to have your data erased, that right stands regardless of anything written here: write to pro@equistaff.com and we will deal with it individually.

Chat conversations with the assistant are an exception in the other direction. Those are kept for 60 days and then removed from your inbox, and we tell you ten days before that happens.

Ending things

You can stop using EquiStaff at any time, and put your account to sleep from your settings. We can close an account that is being used to mislead people, to scrape the site, or to go around a recruiter on a managed search.

A subscription runs to the end of the period you have paid for. Cancelling stops the next renewal; it does not refund the period you are in.
