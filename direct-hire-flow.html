Recruiter-managed search

How a direct-hire search works

Every client and every candidate can come from the site or from outside it — a phone call, an email, a referral. Each step below shows both sides of the same moment: what the recruiting team does, and what the client sees.

Recruiting team does (admin)Client sees
  1. 1

    Add the client

    Recruiting team doesDesigned in the admin portal (next)
    From a site inquiryA Request talent form or the assistant creates the client record. The recruiter checks it and opens it.

    OR

    Added by handFor leads that come by phone, email or referral: the recruiter types in the business, contact name and email.
    Client sees

    An email invite from EquiStaff: “Your recruiter has set up your account.” One link to set a password — no sign-up form, because the recruiter already has their details.

    Sign in →
  2. 2

    Open the search

    Recruiting team doesDesigned in the admin portal (next)

    Enters the role from the briefing call, or uploads the client’s job description. If the client sent the role through the site, it is already filled in.

    Client sees

    The search appears on Your searches, with a progress track and their recruiter’s note. Clients who start on the site send the role over themselves.

    Request talent →Your searches →
  3. 3

    Present candidates

    Recruiting team doesDesigned in the admin portal (next)
    Pick someone already on EquiStaffSearch seekers, add them to the shortlist, write the EquiStaff feedback and log the reference checks.

    OR

    Add a candidate from a resumeUpload the resume and write the summary. The candidate gets an invite to claim their profile. Until they do, the client sees only the recruiter’s summary and the resume.
    Client sees

    A new person on the shortlist, marked Presented with the date. Contact details stay hidden until the placement is settled.

    The shortlist →
  4. 4

    Client reviews

    Recruiting team doesDesigned in the admin portal (next)

    Gets the client’s rating and written feedback on each person as it is sent, and uses it to adjust who comes next.

    Client sees

    Opens each candidate: resume first, then EquiStaff’s feedback, experience, skills and references. Rates them if they like, and writes feedback — required before they can decide.

    A candidate →
  5. 5

    Interview, offer, hired

    Recruiting team doesDesigned in the admin portal (next)

    Sees every date as it is logged, and marks the hire. Releases contact details once the placement is settled.

    Client sees

    Moves people to interview, second interview and offer; each step is dated on the candidate’s Activity. A step that sits more than three days with nothing after it turns red: 6 days since presented

    See a flagged candidate →
  6. 6

    Status flows back to the recruiter

    Recruiting team doesDesigned in the admin portal (next)

    Every client decision, rating, note and date lands on the recruiter’s admin dashboard, so they can see which searches are moving and which have stalled — without chasing anyone by email.

    Client sees

    Nothing extra to do. Their shortlist shows each person’s status, and the search track moves from Presented through to Hired.
