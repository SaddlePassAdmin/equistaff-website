Barn Manager

Golden Ocala Equestrian · Ocala, Florida

$55,000 – $68,000 · housing included

Full time 24 horses · hunter/jumper Listing verified 3 Oct REF 10480 · posted 3 days ago

About the role

Golden Ocala is looking for a barn manager to run the day-to-day of a 24-horse hunter/jumper operation. You'd be leading a team of four — two grooms, a working student and a part-time rider — and reporting directly to the owner.

This is a role for someone who has done it before. The horses are competing most weekends through the winter season, so the barn runs to a schedule and the owner wants someone who can hold it without being asked twice.

What you'd be doing

  • Running the daily schedule — turnout, feed, farrier, vet and training
  • Managing and rostering a team of four
  • Keeping health and competition records up to date
  • Ordering feed, bedding and supplies against a monthly budget
  • Being the point of contact for the owner's trainer and the vet

What they're looking for

  • Three or more years managing a barn of a comparable size
  • Comfortable handling young and competition horses
  • Someone who communicates early rather than after the fact
  • Clean driving licence; towing experience is a plus, not a requirement

The barn

Golden Ocala Equestrian sits ten minutes from the World Equestrian Center. Twenty-four stalls, two arenas, a covered round pen and direct hack access. The owner has had the same vet and farrier for eleven years, which tells you most of what you need to know about how the place is run.
