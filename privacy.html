Short, specific, and written so you can actually tell what happens to what you give us.

What we collect

If you browse, almost nothing: the pages you look at and standard server logs. Browsing never requires an account, and we do not ask you to create one to see a job.

If you create a profile, we hold what you give us — name, contact details, work history, disciplines, availability, housing needs, pay expectations, and any resume you upload or that we help you build.

If you are an employer, we hold your business details, your job posts, and the briefs you send us.

We do not ask about family status or dependents. Where we need to know about housing, we ask who — people or pets — will need housing, which gets the same answer without straying into territory that creates hiring-discrimination risk.

Why we collect it

  • To show you roles that suit, and to let employers find people who suit them
  • To let the recruiting team represent you properly, which is the service
  • To run your account: signing in, resetting a password, billing an employer
  • To tell you about roles, if you asked us to

Who sees it

An employer sees your profile when you apply to them, or when you agree we can put you forward. Not before. Your profile is not browsable by employers who have not been introduced to you.

If you mark yourself as looking quietly, your profile is hidden from every employer search, not only your current employer.

Feedback an employer gives about you goes to the recruiter only. It is never shown to you and is not kept on your profile. That is deliberate — an employer who thinks a candidate will read it stops saying anything useful.

Some roles are synced from SmartSearch, EquiStaff's applicant tracking system. Applying to one of those may pass your application into SmartSearch so a recruiter can work it.

The assistant, and your chat history

The site has an AI assistant. It is an assistant, not a person, and it says so if you ask.

We keep chat conversations for 60 days, so you can scroll back to what was said, and so the recruiting team can pick up a thread without making you repeat yourself. After 60 days a conversation is removed from your inbox.

We warn you first. Ten days before a conversation reaches 60 days old, we tell you in the message itself, so there is time to save anything you want to keep. We never remove a conversation silently.

If a conversation leads to an application or a placement, the parts that matter to that record are kept with the record rather than with the chat, and the retention below applies to them instead.

The assistant will not tell you another person’s details, and it will not quote a recruiting fee. Those go to a human every time.

Your choices

  • Ask for a copy of everything we hold about you
  • Correct anything that is wrong, from your profile, at any time
  • Hide yourself from employer searches with “looking quietly”
  • Put your account to sleep, which hides your profile and stops all email
  • Turn off job alerts without closing your account
  • Download your resume and your application history

How long we keep it, and what happens when you leave

This is the part people skim, so it is written plainly.

We keep your record. Putting your account to sleep hides your profile from every employer search, withdraws you from anything still open and stops all email. It does not erase your history with us. Your profile, your applications and the notes from any conversation you had with the recruiting team stay on file.

Why. Two reasons, and both are honest. If you come back, nothing has to be rebuilt. And EquiStaff has been placing people since 1982 — the recruiting team needs to know they have spoken to you before, what you were looking for, and how it went. That memory is the service.

Records of placements, invoices and anything else we need for tax or legal reasons are kept for as long as the law requires, whatever else happens to your account.

If you have a legal right to erasure — residents of California and some other places do — write to pro@equistaff.com and we will deal with your request individually and tell you what we can and cannot remove.

Questions about any of this: contact us, or write to EquiStaff, Ocala, Florida.
