Housing in a job offer: the questions to ask

Draft · last saved 2 minutes ago

Preview Save draft Publish

Most equine jobs come with somewhere to live, and the housing can matter as much as the pay. Before you say yes, ask these questions.

Is it private or shared?

A room in a shared house is very different from your own apartment over the barn. Ask who else lives there and whether the barn's night checks happen from it.

What's included?

Utilities, internet and laundry are often included, but not always. Ask for it in writing with the offer.

Can anyone come with you?

If a partner, a dog or a horse of your own is coming too, say so early.
