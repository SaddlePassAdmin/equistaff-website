Kaitlin's takeSpoke to Pete on 2 October · 50 minutes

Pete has carried a seven-state territory in animal health for six years and grew it from $2.6M to $4.2M. That is the closest thing on this shortlist to the job you are actually advertising, and they have done it selling into the same barns and distributors you need to reach.

What makes Pete worth your time over a straight consumer-goods salesperson: they grew up on an eventing yard in Versailles and still compete at preliminary. They know why a barn manager will not switch feed in the middle of a show season. You will not spend the first six months explaining the customer to them.

They are leaving because their territory was split in a reorganisation and half the accounts they built went to someone else. I checked that with two people. One thing to know going in: they will ask about how the commission structure handles a long sales cycle, and they will ask it early. It is the right question and I would take it as a sign they have been paid badly for patient work before.
