Everything built so far. These are real working pages — click into them, scroll them, fill the forms in. The landing decision comes first; pick one and the rest follow it.

Where this stands — 21 October

  • The employer name is now confidential on recruiter-managed roles. The advert carries the barn’s character, not its identity. Roles an employer posted themselves still show their name — that contrast is what makes the Recruiter-managed badge mean something. See Job detail, and the tracker on Profile where the name appears once you are shortlisted.
  • AI concierge — five V1 states, each citing the hard rule it enforces, plus the Phase 2 nudge kept separate. V1 is a scripted assistant, not a self-learning one. Built against EquiStaff Chatbot: Engineering Handoff (V1). See AI concierge.
  • Candidate profile — the screen behind every View button on the shortlist. The recruiter’s judgement first, then the phone-checked references that support it, then the decision. See Candidate profile.
  • Resume builder — plain questions on the left, the document assembling itself on the right. For the candidate who has never written one.
  • Pricing is now Kaitlin’s real price list — $50 a month, $140 a quarter, $460 a year, and direct hire at $1,000 up front with the balance on the hire’s first day. The percentage is never stated. Only the middle tier is still unpriced.
  • Closing out a candidate now requires a star rating, and it goes to the recruiter only. See Searches & shortlist.
  • Option A is the final landing page. Two calls to action above the fold instead of four. B is the documented alternate; C is retired.
  • Numbers are legible now — IBM Plex Mono, larger and bolder, replacing Cutive Mono which had no bold weight.
  • The portal is brighter, and contrast was measured rather than eyeballed — white sits at 13.5:1 and 9.0:1 on the two new grounds.
  • The portal is no longer one flat column — applications differ by status, and sections have room to breathe.
