Thirty-three screens, three journeys.

These are real working pages, not pictures of pages. Click into them, scroll them, fill the forms in — the prototype remembers what you do. Start with a journey rather than a screen list; each one runs in order, the way a real person would move through it.

Deliberately not built yet

  • Kaitlin’s own admin portal — where she runs searches, holds candidates she already knows, writes the shortlist up and tracks invoices. Agreed out of scope on 7 October. The employer-side rating and progress patterns are the groundwork for it.
  • Anything that needs the live database — blocked on Azure access and the SmartSearch API terms.
  • Logo, final typeface and iconography — with Brittany.

The prototype remembers what you do. Apply for a role and it shows up on the profile. Save a listing and it’s in saved roles. Publish a post and it’s on the job posts screen.

Two things worth doing while you’re in here

  • Drag the window narrower to see the phone version. Everything rearranges; the portal navigation scrolls sideways rather than disappearing.
  • Follow a journey in order rather than jumping around. Steps 1 to 5 of journey one are the ones that carry the most change from the live site.
