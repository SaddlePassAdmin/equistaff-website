Putting your account to sleep

Deactivating hides your profile from every employer search, stops all email, and withdraws you from anything still open. You can wake it up whenever you like and pick up where you left off.

We keep your record. Your profile, applications and work history stay on file so that nothing is lost if you come back, and so the recruiting team can still see that they have spoken to you before. Our privacy policy sets out exactly what is kept and for how long.

Live somewhere with a legal right to erasure, such as California? Write to pro@equistaff.com and we will handle it individually.
